Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about SpeakUp & Learn

How does the AI feedback work?

Our advanced AI analyzes your speech patterns, pace, clarity, and confidence levels to provide personalized feedback. It identifies areas for improvement and tracks your progress over time.

Is my data secure and private?

Absolutely! We use enterprise-grade encryption to protect your recordings and personal data. Your audio files are processed securely and never shared without your consent.

Can I use this for professional presentations?

Yes! Many professionals use SpeakUp & Learn to prepare for important presentations, job interviews, and public speaking engagements. Our challenges are designed for real-world scenarios.

Do I need any special equipment?

Just your computer's built-in microphone is enough to get started! For the best experience, we recommend using headphones to hear your recordings clearly.

How long does it take to see results?

Most users notice improved confidence within 2-3 weeks of regular practice. Significant improvements in speaking skills typically occur within 1-2 months.

Can I cancel my subscription anytime?

Yes, you can cancel your subscription at any time. You'll continue to have access to your account until the end of your current billing period.
